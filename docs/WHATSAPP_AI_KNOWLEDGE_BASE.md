## 3. PRICING STRUCTURES

### 3.1 Standard Rental Pricing Formula

```
IF totalDays >= discountThreshold:
    dailyRate = discountedRate
ELSE:
    dailyRate = standardDailyRate

totalCost = dailyRate × totalDays
```

Day counting is **inclusive** — both start date and end date count. Same-day rental = 1 day.

## 9. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)

**Q: How far in advance should I book?**
Book 3–7 days in advance for best availability. Same-day bookings sometimes possible — contact us to check.

**Q: What is your cancellation policy?**
24+ hours before rental = full refund. Less than 24 hours = 50% charge. No refunds for early returns.

**Q: Can I extend my rental period?**
Yes, subject to availability. Contact us ASAP. Additional charges apply at standard daily rates.

**Q: What are your rental rates?**
Starting from RM50/day. Multi-day rentals get discounted rates from 3–4+ days onward depending on camera.

**Q: Do you offer weekly or monthly rates?**
We offer bulk discounts for longer rentals (3+ or 4+ days depending on the camera). Contact us via WhatsApp to discuss longer rental durations.

**Q: Is there a security deposit?**
Yes — a RM100 refundable security deposit is required for all rentals. Fully refunded upon safe return of the equipment in good condition.

**Q: What accessories are included?**
All rentals include essential accessories: memory cards, batteries, chargers, carrying cases. See camera-specific "Included" lists above for full details.

**Q: What if the equipment gets damaged?**
Minor wear is expected. Significant damage: repair costs deducted from deposit. Full replacement cost (up to RM3,600) if beyond repair or lost.

**Q: Can I test the equipment before renting?**
Yes — equipment demo provided at pickup. Our team demonstrates all features.

**Q: Do you offer delivery and pickup?**
Yes — Lalamove/GrabExpress delivery within KL and surrounding areas. Self-pickup also available at No 78, Jalan Masjid, Selayang Pandang.

**Q: What are your pickup/return hours?**
Mon–Fri 9AM–8PM, Sat–Sun 10AM–6PM. Special arrangements possible with advance notice.

**Q: Can someone else pickup/return for me?**
Yes — must present a copy of your ID, booking confirmation, and written/WhatsApp authorization from you.

**Q: What if I need technical support during my rental?**
Full WhatsApp support during business hours. Phone support and equipment replacement available for urgent issues.

**Q: Do you rent to international visitors?**
Yes — valid passport and credit card for security deposit. Book in advance and confirm pickup arrangements.

**Q: Can I rent multiple cameras at once?**
Yes — package deals available for multiple camera rentals. Use the Rental Kit feature on the website or contact us for custom pricing.
