## 👤 Customer Guide

### 🎬 Camera Rental

#### Step 1: Browse Available Cameras
- Visit [captura.my](https://captura.my)
- Browse the camera catalog on the homepage
- View available cameras with specifications and pricing
- Click on a camera to see more details

#### Step 2: Select Your Rental Dates
- Click "Reserve Now" on any camera
- Use the interactive calendar to select your pickup and return dates
- The system shows availability in real-time
- If dates are unavailable, you'll see a notification

#### Step 3: Choose Pickup Method
Select one of the following:
- **Shop Pickup**: Pick up in person at our location
  - **Time**: After 9:30 PM (day before rental start)
- **Home Delivery**: We deliver to your location
  - Additional RM20 charge applies

#### Step 4: Provide Your Details
Fill in the required information:
- **Full Name**
- **Email Address**
- **Phone Number**
- **IC Number** (Malaysian citizens)
- **Delivery Address** (if choosing home delivery)

#### Step 5: Accept Terms & Submit
- Review the rental terms and conditions
- Click "Accept" to confirm
- Submit your booking
- You'll receive a confirmation email immediately

## 🔧 Admin Guide

### 🌐 Access the Admin Dashboard

1. Visit [https://captura.my/admin/login](https://captura.my/admin/login)
2. Enter your admin username and password
3. Click "Login"

### 📊 Dashboard Overview

After logging in, you'll see:
- **Statistics Cards**: Total bookings, revenue, upcoming pickups/returns
- **Recent Activity**: Latest bookings and updates
- **Quick Actions**: Approve bookings, send reminders

### 📅 Booking Management

#### Pending Approvals
1. Go to "Booking Approvals" tab
2. Review each pending booking
3. **Approve** or **Reject** the booking
4. Click "Confirm" to approve
5. The customer will receive an email notification

#### All Bookings
1. Go to "Bookings" tab
2. View all bookings in a table format
3. Filter by status (Confirmed, Cancelled, Completed)
4. Search by customer name, email, or phone
5. Click on a booking to view details

### 🎞️ Camera Management

#### Add New Camera
1. Go to "Cameras" tab
2. Click "Add Camera"
3. Fill in camera details:
   - Name (e.g., "DJI Action 5 Pro")
   - Description
   - Image URL
   - Daily, Weekly, Monthly rates
   - Deposit amount
   - Specifications
4. Save the camera

#### Update Camera Information
1. Find the camera in the list
2. Click "Edit"
3. Make changes to any field
4. Save updates

### 👥 Customer Management

1. Go to "Customers" tab
2. View all registered customers
3. Click on a customer to see:
   - Contact information
   - Booking history
   - Past services

### 📧 Email Notifications

#### Send Reminders
- **Pickup Reminder**: Automatically sent the day before pickup
- **Return Reminder**: Automatically sent on return date
- **Thank You Email**: Sent immediately after booking confirmation

### 🔔 Push Notifications (for Admin)

1. Install the PWA app on your mobile:
   - Open admin dashboard on mobile
   - Click "Add to Home Screen"
2. Enable push notifications:
   - Toggle "Enable Notifications" in admin
   - Allow browser notifications
3. You'll receive alerts for:
   - New bookings
   - Upcoming pickups
   - Upcoming returns

### 🎯 Quick Actions

- **Mark as Returned**: Update booking status to "Completed"
- **Generate Invoice**: Create PDF invoices for customers
- **Send WhatsApp Message**: Quick chat for pickups/returns
- **View Calendar**: See all bookings on a visual timeline

### 🔐 Account Security

- Change your password regularly
- Use strong, unique passwords
- Do not share your admin credentials

## ❓ FAQ

### Customer Questions

**Q: What is the rental deposit?**  
A: The deposit is a refundable security deposit required when you pick up the equipment. It varies by camera and is returned after the rental period is complete, provided the equipment is in good condition.

**Q: What happens if I need to cancel?**  
A: You can cancel your booking by contacting us via email or WhatsApp. If you cancel at least 24 hours before pickup, you may be eligible for a full refund of your deposit.

**Q: Can I extend my rental?**  
A: Yes, you can extend your rental period. Contact us at least 24 hours before your return date, and we'll arrange a date extension for you.

**Q: What if the camera is damaged?**  
A: If any damage occurs beyond normal wear and tear, you may be charged for repairs. Please notify us immediately if any damage is discovered.

**Q: Is insurance included?**  
A: Basic insurance is included. For valuable items like professional cameras, we recommend additional insurance coverage through your personal insurance.

### Admin Questions

**Q: How do I add a new camera?**  
A: Go to the "Cameras" tab and click "Add Camera". Fill in all the details including pricing and specifications.

**Q: Can I set up automatic booking approvals?**  
A: Currently, all bookings require manual approval. You can approve multiple bookings at once using the "Bulk Approve" feature.

**Q: How do I generate invoices?**  
A: Go to the "Bookings" tab, find the booking you want to invoice, and click "Generate Invoice". A PDF will be created automatically.

**Q: What does each booking status mean?**  
A:
- **Pending**: Waiting for your approval
- **Confirmed**: Approved and confirmed
- **Cancelled**: Cancelled by customer or admin
- **Completed**: Equipment has been returned

**Q: How do I update prices?**  
A: Go to the "Cameras" tab, edit any camera, and update the daily/weekly/monthly rates. Changes apply to all future bookings.
